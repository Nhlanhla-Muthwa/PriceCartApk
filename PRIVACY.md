PriceCart Privacy Policy

PriceCart is an offline, privacy-first mobile application.

Data Collection: PriceCart does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, analytics, or user information.

Camera & Photos: Camera access is strictly used locally on your device for real-time Optical Character Recognition (OCR) price tag scanning and item photography. No images or camera feeds leave your device.

Offline Storage: All shopping lists, history, and app preferences are stored strictly on your local device storage using an offline SQLite database.
